# Helpdesk & Customer Support Ticketing SaaS

## 1. Project Title

**Helpdesk & Customer Support Ticketing SaaS**

## 2. Domain

**Customer Support & Service Management**

The system is designed to centralize customer support operations by providing a structured platform for creating, assigning, tracking, resolving, and managing customer support tickets.

## 3. User Types

The system will support three primary user types:

### 1. Customer

Customers can create support tickets, provide issue details, communicate with support agents, and track the progress and resolution of their requests.

### 2. Support Agent

Support agents can view and manage assigned tickets, communicate with customers, update ticket status, prioritize work, and resolve customer issues.

### 3. Administrator

Administrators can manage users and support agents, configure ticket categories and priorities, monitor support activities, and access system-level reports and analytics.

## 4. Problem Statement

Many organizations manage customer support requests through scattered emails, phone calls, messaging platforms, or spreadsheets. This makes it difficult to consistently track support requests, assign tickets to the appropriate support agents, monitor response and resolution times, and maintain a complete history of customer interactions.

Support teams may also lack centralized visibility into ticket workload, priorities, recurring issues, and agent performance. Customers may have limited visibility into the progress of their requests and may need to repeatedly contact the organization for updates.

Therefore, there is a need for a centralized customer support platform that organizes support requests into trackable tickets, provides role-based workflows, maintains communication history, and gives administrators visibility into support operations.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a web-based **Helpdesk & Customer Support Ticketing SaaS** that provides a centralized platform for managing customer support requests.

Customers will be able to create and track support tickets. Support agents will be able to view assigned tickets, communicate with customers, update ticket statuses, and resolve issues. Administrators will be able to manage users, agents, categories, priorities, assignments, and support activities.

The system will implement a structured ticket lifecycle:

**Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed**

The platform will maintain ticket comments, assignments, notifications, and activity history to provide transparency and traceability throughout the support process.

The system will also provide dashboards and analytics to help administrators and support teams understand ticket volume, priorities, resolution status, and support workload.

The architecture will provide natural scope for a future AI-based enhancement without making advanced AI functionality a dependency of the initial MVP.

## 6. Core Entities

The system will contain the following core entities:

1. **User** — stores customer, support-agent, and administrator account information.
2. **Role** — defines the permissions and access level of each user.
3. **Ticket** — represents a customer support request and its current lifecycle status.
4. **Category** — classifies tickets according to the type of customer issue.
5. **Priority** — defines the urgency level of a ticket.
6. **Ticket Comment** — stores communication between customers and support agents within a ticket.
7. **Ticket Assignment** — records which support agent is responsible for a ticket.
8. **Notification** — stores notifications generated by important ticket and account events.
9. **Knowledge Base Article** — stores reusable support information and solutions for common customer issues.
10. **Activity Log** — records important actions and changes performed within the system for traceability.

These entities have meaningful relationships and support the application's business workflows rather than functioning as isolated lookup tables.

## 7. User Roles & Responsibilities

| Role              | Responsibilities                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customer**      | Register/login, create tickets, view own tickets, add comments, track status, and close/confirm resolved tickets             |
| **Support Agent** | View assigned tickets, update status, add responses, manage ticket resolution, and communicate with customers                |
| **Administrator** | Manage users and agents, configure categories and priorities, assign tickets, monitor activities, and view support analytics |

Role-based authorization will ensure that users can access only the operations permitted for their role.

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

1. Customers can securely register/login and create support tickets.
2. Customers can view and track the status of their own tickets.
3. Support agents can access assigned tickets and communicate with customers.
4. Support agents can update ticket status and resolve support requests.
5. Administrators can manage users, agents, categories, and priorities.
6. The ticket lifecycle follows controlled status transitions.
7. Role-based authentication and authorization are enforced.
8. Ticket, comment, assignment, notification, and activity information is persisted in the database.
9. Important ticket activities can be traced through activity history.
10. The application provides a responsive web interface for desktop and mobile screen sizes.
11. The application can be tested and deployed as a production-ready web application.

## 9. Out of Scope

The following features are outside the scope of the initial project:

* Native Android or iOS applications
* Voice-based customer support
* Built-in payment processing
* Full call-center or telephony integration
* Social-media support integration
* Multi-company enterprise tenancy
* Advanced AI automation in the initial MVP
* Workforce and payroll management
* Replacement of full-scale enterprise CRM systems

Advanced AI functionality may be considered later as the project's enhancement feature, provided that it is integrated into the same live product.

## 10. Chosen Technology Track

**Track:** Python — FastAPI

### Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** React
* **Backend:** Python + FastAPI
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
* **ORM:** SQLAlchemy
* **Authentication:** JWT
* **API Documentation:** OpenAPI / Swagger
* **Testing:** Pytest
* **Version Control:** Git + GitHub
* **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions

The final cloud hosting platforms will be selected during the deployment phase based on the project's requirements.

## Project Scope Summary

The Helpdesk & Customer Support Ticketing SaaS will provide a centralized platform for customer support management with three primary roles: **Customer, Support Agent, and Administrator**. The system will manage the complete lifecycle of customer support tickets while maintaining communication, assignments, notifications, and activity history.

The project is designed to provide meaningful business workflows beyond basic CRUD operations and will provide a foundation for a future AI-based enhancement.
